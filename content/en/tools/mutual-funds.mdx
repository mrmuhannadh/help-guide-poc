    ## Filter Details

    - **Filter by Symbol** – Filter details by clicking on the **Filter** text box and selecting the **Symbol**.
    - **Filter by Country** – Filter by country by selecting the country drop down.
    - **Filter by Currency** – Filter by currency by selecting the currency drop down.
    - **Filter by Asset Type** – Filter by asset type by selecting the asset type drop down.
