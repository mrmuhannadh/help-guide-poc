## Download & Export Details

**STEPS**

i. Tick on the records you wish to download.  
ii. Click on the **Download** button at the bottom of the window or click on the download icon in front of the record.  
iii. Once the record is downloaded successfully, the icon will change from download icon to Tick icon.  
iv. Click on the export icon to export record as an Excel document.
