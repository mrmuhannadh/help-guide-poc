# ReactionWithVolume

SYNTAX ReactionWithVol()

This will return a +1 whenever a reaction with volume occurs.  
It will continue to return this same value until either a Rally, an Inside day, or an Outside day occur.  
At any time where ReactionWithVol() is not equal to +1, it will be equal to 0.

A reaction with volume occurs when:

- today's high is less than or equal to the previous day's high  
- today's low is less than the previous day's low  
- **today's volume is greater than the previous day's volume**

Eg: ReactionWithVol();
