## Choose Symbols

STEPS
1. Click the **Symbols** dropdown.  
2. Under **Add Symbols**, type the symbol or description you wish to add.  
3. You may add several symbols.  
4. To deselect an added symbol, un-check it.  
5. To remove an added symbol, click the remove icon next to it.  
6. To search within added symbols, type in the **Filter** search box.  
7. Click **Save** to apply the selection.

## Filter Details

STEPS
1. Click the **Actions** dropdown.  
2. Select the actions you want by checking the boxes.  
3. To search actions, type in the **Filter** search box.  
4. Click **Save** to apply the selected criteria.

## Right-click Options

1. Right-click a record and invoke the market function you require (e.g. Depth By Price, Graph, Time and Sales).  
2. For other common right-click actions see the "Right-click Options" section in Getting Started.  
3. Results can be exported as Excel, or copied (with headers, selection, or current cell).
