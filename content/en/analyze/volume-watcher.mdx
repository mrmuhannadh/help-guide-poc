## Filter Details

• **Filter by Market** – Filter details by market using the market filter.  
• **Filter by Sector** – Filter details by sector via the Sector dropdown.  
• **Filter by Criteria** – Filter details by criteria via the Criteria dropdown.  
• **Filter Trigger Factor** – Filter by Trigger Factor by entering a value in the text box.

## Right-click Options

1. Right-click a record to invoke market functions (e.g. Depth By Price, Graph, Time and Sales).  
2. For other common right-click options see "Right-click Options" in Getting Started.  
3. Results can be exported to Excel or copied (with headers, selection, or current cell).
