## Filter Details

• **Filter by Intraday/History** – Choose Intraday or History data.  
• **Filter by Symbol** – Enter the symbol in the symbol text box.  
• **Filter by Market** – Filter by market using the market filter.  
• **Filter by Sector** – Filter by sector using the Sector dropdown.  
• **Filter by Period** – When History is selected, choose the Period to display.

## Right-click Options

1. Right-click a record and invoke market functions (e.g. Depth By Price, Graph, Time and Sales).  
2. For other common right-click options see "Right-click Options" under Getting Started.  
3. Results can be exported to Excel or copied (with headers, selection, or current cell).
