# Price Ticker

<FigureProvider>
    The Price Ticker will be displayed once the Market is open, at the top or bottom of the
    application window based on where it was setup, as in *Figure 1*.

    The Price Ticker will display the following details:

    - Symbol name  
    - Quantity @ traded price  
    - Change / Change %  

    If you click on a symbol in the ticker, it will open the Detail Quote of that symbol.

    ## **Ticker Right-click Options**

    **STEPS**

    1. Right-click anywhere on the Price/News Ticker panel to view the below options,  
    as in *Figure 3 & 4*.  
    If the market is closed, you can still right-click and view these:

    - **Ticker Settings** – Edit ticker settings.  
    - **Add New Price Ticker** – Add a new Price Ticker.  
    - **Add New News Ticker** – Add a new News Ticker.  
    - **Close Ticker** – Close the ticker panel.  
    - **Move Down** – Move the *Price/News Ticker* one level down.  
    - **Move Up** – Move the *Price/News Ticker* one level up.  
    - **Market Windows** – Open market windows like DBP, Graph, T&S, Detail Quote,  
        as in *Figure 2*.

    <Figure
        baseName="settings/price-ticker/price-ticker-right-click"
        alt="Price Ticker Right Click Options"
        description="Price ticker right-click options"
    />

    ## **Ticker Settings**

    **STEPS**

    1. To update ticker settings, right-click anywhere on the ticker panel  
    and click **_Ticker Settings_** as in *Figure 4*.

    2. This will open the ticker settings window, as in *Figure 5*.
