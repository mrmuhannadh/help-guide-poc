# News Ticker

The News Ticker will be displayed once the Market is open, at the top or bottom of the application window based on where it was setup. 
The News Ticker will display both **News** displayed and **Announcements** displayed. If you click on a news or announcement symbol in the ticker, it will open the respective Announcement or News in a window.
