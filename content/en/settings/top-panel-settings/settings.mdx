# Settings

<FigureProvider>

1. Click on **Settings → Application Settings** to view the **Settings** window.  
   The following window will be displayed to the user.

2. Manage the top panel settings in this window.

**STEPS**

1. Under the *Market Summary Panel* section, select the **Exchange Title** to be either  
   **Exchange** (Exchange index) or **Description** (Exchange description).

2. Click **Apply** and then the **Save** button.
