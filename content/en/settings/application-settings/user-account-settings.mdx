# User Account Settings

1. Click on **Settings → Application Settings** to view the **Settings** window, the following window will display to the user.
2. Manage user account settings in this window.

    ## Account Details

    **STEPS**

    1. Under the *Account Details* section, you can view your account details such as Username, Expiry Date, and Account Code.
    2. Check the **Remember Username** checkbox to save your Username when logging in.
    3. Click **Apply** and then the **Save** button.

    ## Price

    **STEPS**

    4. Under the *Account Details* section, you can view your real-time price account details such as Username, Expiry Date, and Account Code.
    5. Click **Apply** and then the **Save** button.

    ## Change Password

    **STEPS**

    1. Under the *Change Password* section, enter your **Current Password**, **New Password**, and **Confirm New Password** as shown below.
    2. Click the **Change Password** button.
    3. Click **Apply** and then the **Save** button.
