# Top Panel Settings

1. Click on **Settings → Application Settings** to view the **Settings** window. The following window will be displayed.
2. Manage the top panel settings in this window.

**STEPS**

1. Under the *Market Summary Panel* section, select the **Exchange Title** to be either **Exchange** (Exchange index) or **Description** (Exchange description).
2. Click on **Apply** and then **Save**.
