# General Settings

1. Click on **Settings → Application Settings** to view the **Settings** window, the following window will be displayed to the user.
2. Through the **General Settings** section the user can do these actions: **Close Action**, **Workstation Lock**, and **Clear Cache** as given below.  
   After selecting the below settings click **Apply** and **Save** to apply the settings to the system.
