# View Settings

1. Click on **Settings → Application Settings** to view the **Settings** window. The following window will be displayed:
2. Through the **View Settings** section, the user can set the **language**, **theme**, **layout**, and **time format** settings in this window.

## Change Language

**STEPS**

1. Under the Default Language section, the language can be chosen either **English** or **Arabic** radio buttons.
2. Click **Apply** and then the **Save** button.

## Preferred Theme

**STEPS**

1. Under the **Preferred Theme** section, the theme can be chosen.
2. Click **Apply** and then the **Save** button.

## Change Time Format

**STEPS**

1. Under the **Time Format** section, select from either **12H** or **24H**.
2. Click **Apply** and then the **Save** button.

## Change Date Format

**STEPS**

1. Under the **Date Format** section, select from either **DD/MM/YYYY** or **DD-MM-YYYY** from the dropdown list.
2. Click **Apply** and then the **Save** button.
