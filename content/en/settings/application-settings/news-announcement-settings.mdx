# News and Announcements Settings

1. Click on **Settings → Application Settings** to view the **Settings** window, the following window will display to the user.
2. Manage news and announcement settings in this window.

## Alerts & Indicators

**STEPS**

a. Under the *Alerts & Indicators* section, select the **Pop up New Announcement** checkbox if you want any new announcements to display as a pop-up.

b. Select the News/Announcements indicator to be on: **Symbol**, **Company Code**, **Description**, or **Short Description** as shown below.

  <Figure
    baseName="settings/trade-news-alert"
    alt="Pro 11 Trade News Alert"
    description="Alert Indicators"
  />
</FigureProvider>

c. Click **Apply** and then the **Save** button.

## News & Announcement Languages

**STEPS**

i. Under the *Languages* section, select to display news and announcements in **English**, **Arabic**, or both **English and Arabic**.

ii. Click **Apply** and then the **Save** button.
