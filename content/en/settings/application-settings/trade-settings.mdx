# Trade Settings

1. Click on Settings → Application Settings to view the **Settings** window. The following window will be displayed:

## Trade Alert Settings

**STEPS**

1. There are six sections under **Trade Alert Settings**, namely:  
   **Order Status Alert, Order Status Alert Type, Order List Sorting Alert Type,** and **Show Order Alerts For**.

2. Under the **Order Status Alert** and **Negotiated Deals Alert** sections, select which actions you wish to receive alerts for. (Any number of settings can be selected)

3. Click **Apply** and then the **Save** button.

## Order Ticket Settings

**STEPS**

1. There are two sections under **Trade Alert Settings**, namely:  
   **Order Status Alert** and **Negotiated Deals Alert**.

2. Under the **Order Status Alert** and **Negotiated Deals Alert** sections, select which actions you wish to receive alerts for. (Any number of settings can be selected)

3. Click **Apply** and then the **Save** button.
