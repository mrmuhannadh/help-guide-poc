## Search News & Announcements

**STEPS**

1. Click the **Search** tab to display search options.  
2. Select a **From / To** date or choose a **Predefined Period** (real time, 1 day, 1 week, 1 month, all) to scope the search.  
3. Enter the **Symbol** and/or **Title** (if known).  
4. Tick **Show Announcements** to include announcements in results (untick to omit).  
5. When searching announcements you may select an **Exchange** and **Save**.  
6. Tick **Show News** to include news in results (untick to omit).  
7. When searching news you may select **News Providers** and **Save**.  
8. To limit asset types, select **Asset Type** and **Save**.  
9. After specifying criteria click **Search** to run the search.
