# Announcements

<FigureProvider>

Announcements for a specific symbol can be viewed in this window. By clicking on an announcement, the announcement details will load.

Click the navigation buttons at the bottom of the Announcement Details window to view the previous and next announcement.

Window invoking options:
1. Quote → Announcements  
2. Quote Dashboard → News & Announcements  
3. Right-click a symbol → Announcements  
4. Detail Quote → Announcements tab

/>

## Filter Details

a. **Filter by Symbol** – Filter announcements by symbol by clicking on the filter text box.  
b. **Filter by Announcement Title** – Filter announcements by announcement title by clicking on the filter text box.
