## Filter Details

▪ **Filter by Symbol** – Filter by the symbol filter text box by clicking on it, and selecting or typing the required symbol.  
▪ **Filter by Volume / Turnover** – Filter by *Volume* or *Turnover* by selecting which option you wish to display.  
▪ **Filter by Average Days** – Filter by the *Average Days* by selecting on the drop down, and select the number of days. If a custom number of days are to be specified, select **Custom** and enter the number of days, and click **Show**.
