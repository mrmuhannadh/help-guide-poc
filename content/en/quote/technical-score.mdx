## Filter Details

The Technical Score window can be filtered by the symbol filter text box by clicking on it, and selecting or typing the required symbol.

## Zoom

You can zoom in to the candle graph if needed, by clicking on the **+** icon at the bottom of the chart, as in Figure 3, and click on the **–** icon to zoom out of the candle graph. Clicking on the square in between the **–** and **+** icons will bring the candle graph to its original state.
