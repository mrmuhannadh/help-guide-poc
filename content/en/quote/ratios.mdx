# Ratios

<FigureProvider>

Ratios can be viewed via the Menu and via the Quote Dashboard window.

Window invoking options:
1. Quote → Ratios  
2. Quote Dashboard → Ratios  
3. Right-click a symbol → Ratios

## Filter Details

• **Filter by Symbol** – Filter by symbol by clicking on the filter text box.  
• **Filter by Period Basis** – Filter by calendar or by fiscal period basis by selecting the option.  
• **Filter by Period** – Filter by period by selecting from the drop down.  
• **Filter by Quarter** – Filter by quarter by selecting the From and To quarter drop down.
