# News

<FigureProvider>

News for a specific symbol can be viewed in this window as in Figure 1. By clicking on a news, the news details will load in a new window.

Click the navigation buttons at the bottom of the News Details window to view the previous and next news.

Window invoking options:
1. Quote → News  
2. Quote Dashboard → News & Announcements  
3. Right-click a symbol → News  
4. Detail Quote → News tab

## Filter Details

a. **Filter by Symbol** – Filter news by symbol by clicking on the filter text box.  
b. **Filter by News Title** – Filter news by news title by clicking on the filter text box.
