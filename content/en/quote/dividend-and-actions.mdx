# Dividend and Actions

<FigureProvider>

The Dividend and Actions window displays the raw stock price chart, dividend details, and other corporate actions. Dividend details can be viewed via the Menu and the Quote Dashboard.

Window invoking options:
1. Quote → Dividend and Actions  
2. Quote Dashboard → Dividend

## Filter Details

Dividend details can be filtered by period: 3 year, 5 year, and All.
