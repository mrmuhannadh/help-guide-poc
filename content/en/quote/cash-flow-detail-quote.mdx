# Cash Flow Detail Quote

<FigureProvider>

Cash Flow Detail Quote details can be viewed via the Menu.

Window invoking options:
1. Quote → Cash Flow Detail Quote  
2. Right-click a symbol → Cash Flow Detail Quote

## Filter Details

The Cash Flow Detail Quote window can be filtered by the symbol filter text box by clicking on it, and selecting or typing the required symbol.
