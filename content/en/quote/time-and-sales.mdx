3. Detail Quote → Time and Sales tab

## Filter Details

The Time and Sales window can be filtered using the symbol filter text box — click it and type or select the required symbol.

## Right-click Options

1. Right‑click on a record to invoke market functions (e.g., Depth By Price, Graph, Time and Sales).  
2. For other right‑click options see Getting Started → Right‑click Options.  
3. This can be exported as an Excel, Copied with Headers, Copied selection and Copied current cell.
